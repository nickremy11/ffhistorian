Privacy Policy

What we keep, why, and how to delete it.

Effective November 21, 2026

FF Historian is a small, independently run set of fantasy football tools at ffhistorian.com and its subdomains: League Assist, My Ranks, the Record Book, Playoff Pools and Projections. This policy covers all of them. In short: we keep only what the tools need to work, we don't sell or rent anything, and we don't show ads. The one tracking tool is Google Analytics, which counts visits (see below).

Signing in with Google

If you choose Continue with Google, Google tells us your email address, whether Google has verified it, your name, and an account ID that stays the same each time. We keep:

  • your email address, to sign you in and to link to an existing account with the same email;
  • your first name and last initial (for example "Jordan T."), as your starting display name, which you can change;
  • Google's account ID, so the same Google account signs in to the same FF Historian account.

Google's sign-in permission also covers your profile picture; we don't read it or store it, and we don't keep your full last name. We request no other Google data and no access to Gmail, Drive, Contacts or anything else. FF Historian's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models.

Fantasy league data

The tools read league data (rosters, scores, standings, settings) from Sleeper and ESPN to show it to you. This is your leagues' data as those platforms already show it to league members. Some of it is cached briefly to keep pages fast. Player values, rankings and projections come from public sources such as FantasyCalc and FantasyPros.

Ask Madden and reading ranking sheets

Two optional features use AI models run by Cloudflare (Workers AI): the Ask Madden chat, and reading a ranking sheet from an uploaded image. What you send (your question and the league data the chat looks up, or the image) is processed by the model, and each request is logged in Cloudflare's AI Gateway, labelled with your account ID, email and Sleeper username, so problems can be investigated. The chat history itself is kept only in your browser tab. This content is not used to train models.

Usage analytics

ffhistorian.com and its tool sites use Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to count visits and see which pages and tools get used. It sets Google Analytics cookies (_ga and _ga_…), and Google receives your IP address, browser and device type and the pages you view. We don't send it your account, email or league data, and we use it only to understand how the site is used, not for advertising. You can opt out with Google's opt-out add-on or any tracker blocker; the tools work the same either way.

Things your browser loads from other services

Like most websites, some pages load files directly from other services, which then see your IP address and browser: fonts from Google Fonts, a drag-and-drop script from jsDelivr (My Ranks), player and team images from Sleeper and ESPN, and college player details from ESPN (devy leagues).

Cookies and browser storage

  • sh_session: keeps you signed in across ffhistorian.com and its subdomains (30 days, secure, not readable by scripts).
  • sh_theme: remembers light or dark mode.
  • Your browser's local storage holds conveniences such as the last tab or filter you used, cached results, and, if you aren't signed in, your settings.

Plus the Google Analytics cookies described above. There are no advertising cookies.

Who we share it with

We don't sell, rent or trade personal information. It is shared only with the services that run the site, and only as needed to do so:

  • Cloudflare: hosting, database, storage, email delivery, AI models and security filtering.
  • Sleeper and ESPN: your requests to them, using your token or cookies when you've added them.
  • Google: Google Analytics (above), and your sign-in details only if you use Google sign-in.

We would disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

Account data and saved settings are kept until you delete your account. Draft rooms aren't tied to an account, so deleting yours doesn't remove them; ask us and we will. Sign-in sessions expire after 30 days. Verification codes and reset links expire within 30 minutes. Cloudflare keeps request and AI Gateway logs for a limited period under its own retention rules. Feedback emails stay in our inbox.

Deleting your data

Delete your account any time from your profile. That permanently removes your account and everything saved to it: tokens, ESPN cookies, settings, rankings, ledger and linked Google sign-in. You can also remove a Sleeper token or ESPN cookies on their own by replacing or clearing them in your profile, and revoke Google sign-in from your Google account. To ask what we hold about you, or for anything else, email support@ffhistorian.com.

Security

Everything is served over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed, and Sleeper tokens and ESPN cookies are encrypted at rest. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what we hold to the minimum the tools need.

Children

FF Historian isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has made an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be noted on the site.
